# Prism-Lab
### Commercial Pricing Strategy & Revenue Growth Platform

🔗 **[Explore the Live Platform](https://hongyaqi0611-beep.github.io/Prism-Lab/)**

An analytical pricing and growth strategy engine built to transform raw corporate and market data into structured commercial recommendations. This platform models pricing power dynamics, value migration, and revenue optimization frameworks for executive decision-making.

### Core Focus Areas
* **Pricing Power & Elasticity:** Evaluating margin resilience and unit economics under supply chain and input cost volatility.
* **Commercial Growth Modeling:** Translating quantitative customer and market segmentation into actionable go-to-market pricing tiers.
* **Competitive Positioning:** Benchmarking value capture and monetization strategies across shifting industry landscapes.
